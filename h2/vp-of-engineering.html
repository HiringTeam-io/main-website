The Role

Build the US engineering org from the ground up.

This is a foundational leadership role, not a maintenance one. You'll work directly with H2's CTO, CEO, and Product leadership to set team culture, technical standards, and cross-border collaboration between our US, Japan, and Vietnam teams.

1

Lead execution on web, AI, and platform initiatives for the US engineering team.

2

Translate customer pain points into scalable technical solutions alongside Product and Design.

3

Shape technical strategy and hiring standards with the CTO and global engineering leadership.

4

Lead architectural decisions for web applications, AI workflows, enterprise integrations, and data models.

5

Hire and develop US engineering staff, particularly L3–L4-caliber engineers.

6

Establish development processes that balance speed, quality, and reliability.

7

Build collaboration models across US, Japan, and Vietnam teams with async workflows.

What We're Looking For

Required & preferred experience.

● Required

  • 15+ years of product engineering experience across web, platform, AI, infrastructure, or enterprise software.
  • 10+ years of engineering leadership, including hiring, managing, coaching, and scaling teams.
  • Demonstrated experience building or scaling AI-enabled products.
  • Technical credibility with hands-on leadership capability.
  • Strong cross-functional leadership across Product, Design, GTM, and executive stakeholders.
  • Comfort operating in ambiguity, deciding with incomplete information.

● Preferred

  • Computer vision, image recognition, or construction-drawing/takeoff experience.
  • Background in construction tech, industrial software, robotics, or vertical SaaS.
  • Full-stack expertise across frontend, backend, data, and platform systems.
  • Experience across Japan–US, APAC–US, or other multinational engineering teams.
  • Prior experience scaling engineering at an early-stage or high-growth startup.
  • Building products for complex, non-technical, or field-based users.
