How we work

Most of the work happens before a résumé reaches you.

Search is largely an information problem. We've spent our time building the tooling that keeps our data honest, so the judgment calls get made on something real.

1

Candidate data we've actually verified

Profiles get checked against live sources rather than trusted from a stale record. Someone who changed jobs six months ago doesn't reach you described as though they didn't.

2

Structured notes, not impressions

Every screen produces a consistent written record, so comparisons across a pipeline hold up and feedback arrives while it still matters.

3

Automation on the admin, humans on the judgment

Scheduling, follow-ups, data hygiene and reporting run themselves. The time that frees goes into candidate conversations and calibration with your team.

4

Job pages built for the role

Each search gets a proper page in the client's own branding, rather than a wall of text on a job board that undersells the opportunity.

How we engage

Retained, or embedded in your team.

Retained search for the hires that need to be right — leadership roles, first-of-their-kind hires, the searches where a bad outcome costs a year.

Embedded when you need recruiting capacity inside the team rather than alongside it. We work as part of your hiring function, in your tools and your standups, for as long as the need lasts.

Typically early and growth-stage teams with technical founders, hiring in San Francisco, New York and remote — often building for industries that software has historically ignored. We're comfortable working across time zones and alongside engineering teams that aren't all in one country.
