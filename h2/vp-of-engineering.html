The Opportunity

You will lead H2's US engineering organization through two connected challenges: building a high-performing team at speed and turning complex customer deployments into a repeatable enterprise product.

Our customers need more than document extraction. They need detailed takeoffs, SKU-level bills of materials, and quotes that incorporate product specifications and business rules. You will connect AI capabilities, product engineering, and customer delivery to make these workflows reliable and scalable.

You will own US engineering hiring, organizational development, and execution. You will partner with the CTO on architecture and technical strategy, with Product and Design on customer outcomes, and with the Japan-based engineering leader responsible for our Japan and Vietnam teams on global collaboration.

What You'll Own

  • Building the US engineering organization. Scale the US team from 13 to 40+ across forward-deployed engineering, core engineering, and AI engineering (CV), in NYC, SF, and remote.
  • Hiring and developing talent. Set competency-based hiring standards with the CTO, build effective onboarding, and develop engineers and leaders who own complex projects and mentor others.
  • The core product pivot. Lead the transition from customer-specific development to a shared, reusable core product, bringing field-built capabilities into the platform and reducing enterprise deployment times from 6–12 months to 3–6.
  • Technical strategy and architecture. Set technical direction with the CTO, guide the proprietary model's growth from 2B to 7–9B parameters, and own architecture across web, AI, integrations and data.
  • Engineering culture and best practices. Build a culture of ownership, urgency, technical excellence and customer empathy, with processes that improve delivery and quality without bureaucracy. Make progress, tradeoffs, risks and dependencies visible.
  • Customer-driven execution. Lead US web, AI and platform delivery, turning customer pain points into scalable solutions with Product, Design, Customer Success and Sales, and make sure every deployment strengthens the shared product.
  • The cross-border operating model. Define how US engineering works with Japan and Vietnam: async collaboration, shared standards, clear ownership and clean handoffs across time zones.

What Success Looks Like

  • A strong, growing team. The US team grows from about 13 to 40+ engineers over the next ~6 months, with solid onboarding, clear ownership and capable leads.
  • Faster deployments. Implementations move from 6–12 months toward 3–6, with less custom work per customer.
  • More product reuse. Work built for one customer becomes shared features, integrations and configuration.
  • Dependable production AI. Accuracy, traceability and cost of AI workflows are measured and improving.
  • Predictable execution. Commitments, capacity, risks and quality are visible and managed.
  • One global team. US, Japan and Vietnam work with clear ownership and shared standards.

What We're Looking For

  • 15+ years of product engineering across web, platform, AI, infrastructure or enterprise software.
  • 5+ years of hiring, managing, coaching and scaling engineering teams, including building and leading an engineering organization of 20–40+.
  • Deep product ownership: taking ambiguous customer problems to clear technical direction, and owning the product outcomes that follow.
  • Deep AI engineering experience, building and scaling AI products in production: applied AI, computer vision, LLM workflows, ML systems and AI infrastructure.
  • Leading teams through the full ML lifecycle: data, training, evaluation, deployment and monitoring of models in production.
  • Leading architecture and technical decisions for complex software products.
  • Building development processes that balance speed, quality, reliability and maintainability.
  • Leading across Product, Design, GTM, Customer Success, Recruiting and executives.
  • Clear written and spoken English that creates clarity for US teams and engineering partners in Japan and Vietnam.

Bonus Points

  • Computer vision on drawings, images or scanned documents, or training proprietary foundation models.
  • Construction tech, takeoff and estimating, or vertical SaaS for field-based, non-technical users.
  • Leading multinational teams, especially Japan–US or APAC.
  • Scaling engineering at an early-stage or high-growth startup.
