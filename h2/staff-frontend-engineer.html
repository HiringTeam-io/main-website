The Role: Staff Frontend Engineer | Frontend FDE

Frontend Stack
TypeScript, React.js, Next.js, Vite.js, Canvas, WebGL, SVG
Comp Package
$250K–$350K base, annual cash bonus, equity
Benefits
Health, 401(k), flexible hours, unlimited PTO, personal development stipend + more

This role will own and shape Origami, the custom canvas we're building in-house. A construction estimator opens a drawing, our model drops thousands of detected objects onto it, and they pan, zoom, select and correct inside Origami until the numbers are right. Those numbers become a quote, then an order, then a building. Rendering thousands of interactive objects over a dense drawing, fast enough that an estimator trusts it, is the core engineering problem.

None of the frontend architecture, the platform underneath it, or the engineering standards are settled yet. This role is where they get decided.

We estimate time with customers at somewhere between 5–15%, and there's no travel required for new deployments.

What You'll Own

  • Origami, our in-house canvas. Rendering, hit-testing, selection, editing and annotation at scale.
  • The AI review loop. How an estimator trusts, corrects and commits what the model produced.
  • Performance. Frame budget, memory and load time on drawings carrying thousands of objects.
  • Customer deployments. 0-to-1 features scoped to each new enterprise rollout, prototyped in front of real estimators.
  • Frontend architecture and standards. State model, component boundaries, type safety, testing, design reviews and code review for a growing team.

What We're Looking For

  • 8+ years of software engineering, shipping production-grade code.
  • 5+ years of frontend engineering, fluent in TypeScript.
  • Experience building frontend applications in Next.js or Vite.js (both is ideal).
  • A track record of building and owning complex features end to end.
  • Experience setting frontend architecture and technical direction for a team.
  • Currently hands-on, with a habit of mentoring engineers through code review and design docs.

Bonus Points

  • Experience building canvas-like applications, from scratch or on top of libraries like tldraw, Konva, PixiJS or Three.js.
  • Experience with WebGL and SVG.
  • Document-heavy or annotation products such as PDF viewers, whiteboards, mapping or CAD/BIM.
  • Construction tech, CPQ/ERP or geospatial exposure.

Interview Process

  1. Intro interview. 45 minutes with our VP of Product.
  2. Take-home assessment. Sign an NDA, then three days to complete.
  3. Live technical interview. 90 minutes covering an assessment review, pair programming and system design.
  4. Final round with the CEO. 30–45 minutes.
